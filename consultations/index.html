Heart tests at the clinic

Monitoring tests that are fitted at the clinic and reviewed by Dr Dhall.

Holter monitoring

A small recorder captures every heartbeat while you go about your day — for palpitations, dizziness or fainting.

  • Worn for 24–48 hours
  • Fitted at the clinic
  • Report with consultation
BookPay onlinePay by UPI

24-hour BP monitoring (ABPM)

An arm cuff measures blood pressure day and night, to confirm hypertension or check that treatment works around the clock.

  • Worn for 24 hours
  • Fitted at the clinic
BookPay onlinePay by UPI

Event loop recorder (ELR)

For symptoms that come and go: records your rhythm whenever symptoms occur, over one to several weeks.

  • Worn for 1–2 weeks
  • Fitted at the clinic

How a consultation works

  1. Book

    Send the form below on WhatsApp or call the clinic. You'll get a confirmed time.

  2. Share your reports

    Bring them to the clinic, or send them on WhatsApp before a video call.

  3. Consult

    History, examination, ECG and an honest conversation about what's going on.

  4. Your plan

    Medicines, tests and targets in writing, with a follow-up date.

Request an appointment

The clinic will confirm the time with you on WhatsApp or by phone.

Please bring

  • All previous reports and discharge summaries
  • Angiography or scan CDs
  • Your medicines, or their strips
  • A list of questions

Booking questions

How do I book?

Fill in the form on this page and send it on WhatsApp or by email, or call the clinic. The team confirms a time with you.

Can I pay online?

Online payment links are being set up. For now you can pay at the clinic by cash, card or UPI. For video consultations, payment details are shared when your time is confirmed.

How long is a consultation?

A first consultation usually takes about 30 minutes. Follow-ups are shorter.

Can a family member join?

Yes, please bring someone along — or add them to a video call. Many patients find it helpful.

What if I need to cancel?

Let the clinic know as early as you can on WhatsApp or by phone so the slot can go to someone else.

Is the clinic open on Sundays?

No. The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 4 to 8 pm. In an emergency, call 112 or 108.
